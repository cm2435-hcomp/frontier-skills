---
name: completion-verification
description: Check every acceptance criterion through the final consumer surface before claiming completion.
---

At the start, turn the user's request into a short list of atomic acceptance criteria. Keep the original wording where
precision matters.

Before finishing:

1. Reread the original request, not just your plan or notes.
2. Check every criterion through the surface the user will actually consume: reopen the saved file in its application,
   revisit the browser state, or inspect the final mobile/desktop state as appropriate.
3. Classify each criterion as `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `UNVERIFIED` from direct evidence.
4. Repair `FAIL` items when possible, then check them again.
5. Never translate `UNVERIFIED` into a completion claim. State the limitation if it remains material.

Do not create a ceremonial report unless the user asked for one. The purpose is to change the final check and the
truthfulness of the answer.
